# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of this project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- **Be considerate** – Your work will be used by other people, and you should consider how your actions affect them.
- **Be respectful** – No harassment or discrimination of any kind is tolerated.
- **Be collaborative** – Share knowledge, help others, and foster a welcoming environment.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

For the full version of the Contributor Covenant, see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
